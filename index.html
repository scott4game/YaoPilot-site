Support & connection help

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Independent developer: alpha2z

Compatibility

Requires iOS 17 or later and a Sony TV that supports local REST API and IRCC-IP control with pre-shared-key authentication. Not all Sony models, regions, or firmware are compatible. Other TV brands, infrared control, and movie catalogs inside TV apps are not supported. YaoPilot is independent and is not affiliated with or officially certified by Sony.

Connect

Keep the TV on. Connect the iPhone and TV to the same local network; Ethernet to the same router is fine. Find IP control in TV network settings, select Normal and Pre-Shared Key authentication, and set your own key. Enter the TV’s current local IPv4 address and that key in the app, then allow Local Network permission. Menu names vary by firmware.

Connection problems

Check the TV is on, the address has not changed, and Local Network permission is enabled for YaoPilot. Guest Wi-Fi, device isolation, or some VPN settings may block access. Check the key and use Reconnect. “Command sent” reports a successful request, not independent confirmation of a screen change.

Power and inputs

The top-right power button checks current TV state: if on, it asks before standby; if on standby and reachable, it requests power-on. Deep standby may disable networking; use the physical remote in that case. Simple IP control may help but can increase standby power use. Input selection requires the TV screen to be on. An unplugged TV cannot be turned on.

Contact support

Email otsgame@yahoo.com with the app version, iOS version, TV model, and the visible error. Do not include credentials or other sensitive information. No account or paid support plan is required to contact the developer.

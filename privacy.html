About this policy

This policy covers the YaoPilot iPhone app (遥点 in Simplified Chinese) and this privacy/support site. alpha2z is the independent developer’s public attribution name. The app has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud backend.

Information processed by the app

The app uses the TV brand, local IP address, pre-shared key, and commands to operate your TV on your home network. It reads device status, remote key capabilities, input names, and installed app launchers. These are not uploaded to the developer. TV information and app lists are used in memory; saved connection details are held in this device’s Keychain. Language and vibration preferences are stored locally.

Local communication and security

The supported TV interface uses HTTP, so the pre-shared key travels with requests on the local network without transport encryption. Use a trusted home network and do not expose the TV control port to the internet. The app requests Local Network permission to communicate with your TV. Permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Storage and deletion

Delete the saved TV in Connection settings to remove its Keychain entry and clear in-app connection information. Keychain entries may survive app removal, so delete the connection before uninstalling. The app uses a device-only, unlocked-device Keychain accessibility setting and does not enable iCloud synchronization for these credentials. Preferences remain locally until changed or removed with app data; system backup behavior is controlled by iOS. TV-side configuration and TV accounts are not deleted by the app.

Website hosting

If these pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. This is separate from the app’s local TV control. These pages include no added analytics, advertising, forms, external fonts, or tracking scripts. Consult GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Support email

If you email otsgame@yahoo.com, the developer receives the address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Email providers also process delivery. Send only what is needed for support; do not send TV keys, passwords, home IP addresses, or device serial numbers. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Contact the same address to ask about or request deletion of correspondence.

Updates and questions

This policy will be updated if app or hosting practices change, with the effective date revised. For privacy questions, contact otsgame@yahoo.com. This app does not collect data for advertising or sell user data.
